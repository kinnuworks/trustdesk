# Orrin Freight Devices and Suppliers

Synthetic document for a fictional company, used for demonstration only.

## Laptops and phones
Staff use company laptops supplied by the outside IT provider. Disk encryption is enabled on Mac laptops; some older Windows laptops are not yet encrypted.
Company email can be used on personal phones without device management.

## Incidents
If a security incident affects customer data, the VP of Engineering decides whether and when to inform customers. There is no written incident response procedure.

## Suppliers
Suppliers are chosen by the team that needs them. No one is assigned to review supplier security, and contracts do not include standard security terms.
