# Halden Labs Incident Response Plan

Owner: Security & IT. Synthetic document for a fictional company.

## Documented procedures
This plan documents how Halden Labs detects, triages, contains, investigates and recovers from security incidents. It is tested in a tabletop exercise twice a year.

## Investigation
Halden Labs maintains an internal incident response team with forensics tooling and can conduct investigations without outside help. An external forensics firm is on retainer for major incidents.

## Evidence
To support investigations, security logs are retained for 12 months.

## Customer notification
Affected customers are notified without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of confirming a security incident that affects their data.

## Vulnerability reports
External researchers can report vulnerabilities to security@halden.example. Reports are acknowledged within two business days and triaged by the Security & IT team.
