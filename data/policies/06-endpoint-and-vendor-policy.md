# Halden Labs Endpoint and Vendor Security Policy

Owner: Security & IT. Synthetic document for a fictional company.

## Company devices
All company laptops are managed by the device management platform, have full-disk encryption turned on and run endpoint malware protection.
Phones may access company email only after enrolment in device management, which enforces device encryption and a passcode. Unenrolled or unencrypted mobile devices are blocked.

## Vendors
The Security & IT team reviews every service provider that will store or process customer data before contract signature, and again every year.
Contracts with these providers require them to protect the security and confidentiality of customer data at least to the standard of this program.
