# Halden Labs Information Security Program

Owner: Security & IT. Version 3.2, approved by the executive team.

Halden Labs is a fictional company used for demonstration. This document is synthetic.

## Scope
The security program covers all information Halden Labs processes for customers, including the production platform, internal IT, HR processes and third-party services that store or process customer data.

## Ownership
The Head of Security owns the information security and privacy program and reports to the CTO. The Security & IT team runs day-to-day controls. The Legal & Privacy team owns privacy notices and data processing agreements.

## Standard
The program is aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls, and its control set is mapped so it can be audited against that standard.

## Policy review
All security policies are reviewed at least once per year, and immediately when a significant change in systems, law or risk occurs.

## Audits
Internal security audits are performed quarterly by the Security & IT team and cover the whole program.
An independent third-party auditor performs a SOC 2 Type II audit every year covering all systems used to deliver the service. A management summary of the latest report is shared with customers under NDA.

## Risk management
Risk assessments are reviewed and updated twice per year, and after any major incident.

## Training
All employees complete security and privacy training during onboarding and then every year. Contractors and interns with system access complete the same training before access is granted.

## Personnel
Background checks are performed for employees in Engineering and Customer Support before they are granted access to production systems or customer data. Other roles are checked where local law allows.
Violations of security policy are handled through the HR disciplinary process.
